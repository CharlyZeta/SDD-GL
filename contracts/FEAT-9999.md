## Intent
Validate the end-to-end capabilities, consistency, and resilience of the sdd-gl process framework using a complex escrow lifecycle implementation.

## Use Case
**Actor:** Buyer, Seller, Escrow Agent
**Goal:** Safely exchange funds under conditional release with dispute resolution.

### Main Flow
1. Buyer initializes escrow with Seller address, dispute period (in days), and deposit amount.
2. Buyer deposits the agreed funds into the escrow.
3. Seller fulfills obligations and requests release of funds.
4. Buyer approves release of funds, system fee (1.5%) is deducted, and remaining is sent to Seller.

### Alternative Flows
- AF-01: Dispute period expires without buyer action → Seller can claim funds auto-release.
- AF-02: Refund requested before dispute period expires → Block the refund and raise a dispute.

## Business Rules
- BR-001: Escrow amount must be positive.
- BR-002: System fee of 1.5% is deducted upon release.
- BR-003: Dispute period must be between 1 and 30 days.
- BR-004: Dispute penalty fee of 3% of deposit is deducted and sent to the Seller upon dispute resolution in favor of Seller, returning 97% to the Buyer. Resolution is triggered by the Escrow Agent.

## Acceptance Criteria
- AC-001: GIVEN an escrow GIVEN dispute period of 10 days WHEN release is called THEN system fee is 1.5% and the remaining is transferred to the Seller.
- AC-002: GIVEN an escrow with amount 0 WHEN initialized THEN it fails.

## Entities Affected
- EscrowAccount: seller_address, amount, status, dispute_expiry, fee_collected

## Ambiguity Log
- [x] BR-001 vs AC-002: Resolved by changing AC-002 to expect failure for amount 0.
- [x] BR-004: Resolved by specifying that the 3% penalty is deducted from the escrow deposit, and resolution is triggered by the Escrow Agent via resolveDispute(favorsSeller).
